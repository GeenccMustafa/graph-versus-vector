# Artemis Program

The Artemis program is NASA's ongoing effort to return humans to the Moon and
eventually send them to Mars. It is named after Artemis, the Greek goddess of
the Moon.

Artemis I launched in November 2022. It was the first integrated flight of the
Space Launch System (SLS) rocket and the Orion spacecraft. The SLS was built by
Boeing and is derived from Space Shuttle technology. The Orion crew capsule was
built by Lockheed Martin.

Artemis II is planned to carry four astronauts around the Moon. Unlike the
Apollo program of the 1960s, which used the Saturn V rocket, the Artemis program
relies on the Space Launch System. NASA plans to land the first woman and the
first person of color on the Moon during the Artemis III mission.
