# Apollo Program

The Apollo program was a series of crewed spaceflights carried out by NASA
between 1968 and 1972. Its goal was to land humans on the Moon and bring them
back safely.

Apollo 11 launched on July 16, 1969, on a Saturn V rocket. Neil Armstrong
commanded Apollo 11 and became the first person to walk on the Moon. Buzz
Aldrin was the lunar module pilot of Apollo 11, and Michael Collins piloted the
command module Columbia.

The Saturn V rocket used by the Apollo missions was a super heavy-lift launch
vehicle developed at the Marshall Space Flight Center. Wernher von Braun led the
team that designed the Saturn V. The rocket remains one of the most powerful
vehicles ever flown.
