# Saturn V

The Saturn V is an American super heavy-lift launch vehicle developed by NASA
and used from 1967 to 1973. It was designed under the direction of Wernher von
Braun at the Marshall Space Flight Center in Huntsville, Alabama.

The Saturn V was used for all Apollo Moon missions. It launched Apollo 11, which
carried Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin to the Moon. The rocket had three stages
and stood 110.6 metres tall.

Boeing, North American Aviation, and Douglas Aircraft Company were among the
major contractors that built components of the Saturn V. After the Apollo
program ended, the Saturn V was also used to launch the Skylab space station in
1973.
